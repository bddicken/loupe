Loupe

A free, open-source screenshot and screen recording app for Mac. Capture, mark up, polish and record in seconds.

Download for Mac
Free · Version 1.0.7 · macOS 14+ · Apple Silicon & Intel
Open source on GitHub By @benjdicken

Screenshot and screen recording features

  • Area, window & fullscreen

    Drag to capture, click a window to grab just that window, or adjust the selection before you shoot.

  • Screen recording to MP4 or GIF

    Record an area, a window or the full screen, with a countdown, highlighted clicks, and microphone and computer audio.

  • Markup

    Arrows, lines, shapes, text, highlighter, numbered steps, plus blur and pixelate for anything sensitive.

  • Beautiful backgrounds

    Gradients, solid colors or your own image, with padding, rounded corners and soft shadows, ready to share.

  • Edit & adjust

    Crop with aspect presets, rotate, and tune exposure, contrast, saturation and hue.

  • Quick access

    A thumbnail after every capture or recording: copy, save, edit, or drag straight into another app.

  • History, pins & timer

    Browse recent captures, pin screenshots above other windows, and use a self-timer for menus.

  • Free, native & private

    Open source, built in Swift for macOS, with no account, no subscription, and nothing uploaded.

Keyboard shortcuts

⌥⇧4Capture an area (or click a window)
⌥⇧5Capture an area, adjusting it before capturing
⌥⇧3Capture the full screen
⌥⇧6Capture the previous area again
⌥⇧7Capture with a self-timer
⌥⇧8Record the screen (press again to stop)

Every shortcut can be changed in Loupe’s Settings.

Install

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Loupe into Applications.
  2. Open Loupe. Its icon appears in the menu bar.
  3. On your first capture, allow Loupe in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording, then reopen Loupe.
“Apple could not verify Loupe”?

This build isn’t notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm it the first time:

  • macOS 15 and 26: open Loupe, dismiss the warning, then click Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • macOS 14: right-click Loupe in Applications, choose Open, then confirm.
  • Or in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Loupe.app

Questions

Is Loupe free?

Yes. Loupe is free and open source under the MIT license, with no account, subscription or watermark.

Which Macs does Loupe run on?

Loupe needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later and runs natively on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

Can Loupe record my screen?

Yes. Press ⌥⇧8 to record an area, a window or the full screen, and save it as an MP4 video or an animated GIF. Recordings can include your microphone and computer audio, with clicks highlighted.

Does Loupe replace the built-in macOS screenshot shortcuts?

No. Loupe uses ⌥⇧ shortcuts by default, so the built-in ⌘⇧3, ⌘⇧4 and ⌘⇧5 keep working. You can change every Loupe shortcut in Settings. How Loupe compares to the macOS Screenshot app.

Does Loupe upload my screenshots?

No. Screenshots and recordings stay on your Mac. The only thing Loupe fetches from the internet is the update feed at loupe.pics.

Is Loupe an alternative to CleanShot X?

For capturing, annotating, adding backgrounds and recording MP4 or GIF, yes, and it is free. CleanShot X adds scrolling capture, text recognition (OCR) and cloud sharing, which Loupe doesn't have. Compare Loupe and CleanShot X.
