Choose Loupe if…

  • You want a free screenshot and screen recording app with no license, account or subscription.
  • You’d like to read or build the source yourself. Loupe is written in Swift and MIT licensed.
  • You want screenshots to stay on your Mac. Loupe doesn’t upload anything.
  • You also tweak images: Loupe’s editor adjusts exposure, contrast, saturation, hue and warmth, and crops with aspect presets.

Choose CleanShot X if…

  • You need scrolling capture, OCR, or keystroke and webcam overlays in recordings.
  • You share captures as links through a hosted cloud, or manage a team.
