Loupe vs the macOS Screenshot app

macOS has screenshots built in: ⌘⇧3, ⌘⇧4 and the ⌘⇧5 toolbar. They’re fine for a quick grab. Loupe is free too, and adds the editing, privacy and sharing tools you reach for when a screenshot is going to someone else.

Use both

Loupe doesn’t take over the system shortcuts. Its defaults use ⌥⇧ instead of ⌘⇧, so ⌥⇧4 captures an area in Loupe while ⌘⇧4 still works as before. Every Loupe shortcut can be changed in Settings.
