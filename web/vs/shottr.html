Loupe vs Shottr

Shottr and Loupe are both small, fast screenshot apps for Mac that you can use for free. The biggest difference: Loupe also records your screen to MP4 or GIF, and its source is open.

Choose Loupe if…

  • You want screenshots and screen recordings (MP4 or GIF, with audio and highlighted clicks) in one app.
  • You prefer open source software you can read and build yourself.
  • You like a quick-access thumbnail after every capture, capture history, a self-timer, and re-capturing the previous area.

Choose Shottr if…

  • You need scrolling capture, OCR, or pixel measuring tools.
  • You’re on a Mac older than macOS 14 Sonoma.
