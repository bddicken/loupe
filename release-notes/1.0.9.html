Loupe 1.0.9

  • Settings that fit your screen. Settings is now split into General, Capture, Recording and Shortcuts tabs instead of one long page that could run off the bottom of a laptop screen. The window is never taller than your screen, scrolls if it needs to, and can be resized. It opens on the tab you used last.
  • One-click choices. Image format, recording format, frame rate and the self-timer delay are now buttons you click once, instead of pop-up menus.
