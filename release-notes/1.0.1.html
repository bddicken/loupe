Loupe 1.0.1

  • New look: a wireframe loupe app icon, with a matching menu bar icon.
  • 50 backgrounds: ten new presets, including sand, lilac, ember, flamingo, dot grid and sandstone.
  • Clearer styling: click away from a shape to deselect it. Color and size changes apply only to the selected shape, or to the next one you draw when nothing is selected.
  • Switching to crop mode or opening the background panel no longer resizes the editor window.
  • Loupe now asks for Screen Recording permission on your first capture instead of at every launch.
