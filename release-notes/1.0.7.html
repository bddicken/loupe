Loupe 1.0.7

  • Open images from Finder. Loupe now shows up in Open With for PNG, JPEG, HEIC, TIFF, GIF, WebP and BMP files, and opens them straight in the editor, whether or not Loupe is already running. To make Loupe the default for a file type, select an image, press ⌘I, choose Loupe under Open with, and click Change All. Preview stays the default until you do.
