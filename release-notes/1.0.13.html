Loupe 1.0.13

  • Choose the font and weight of text. A new menu in the editor toolbar sets the weight, from Light to Heavy, and the font: System, Rounded, Serif, Mono, or any font installed on your Mac. It applies to the selected text and to the text you add next, including text you're in the middle of typing.
  • Turn drop shadows on or off. A new toolbar button controls the drop shadow of arrows, lines, rectangles, ellipses, pen strokes, text and counters, for the selected shape and the ones you draw next.
  • Saved images match the editor. The editor showed a shadow behind every shape, but only text and counters had one in the saved image. Shapes now look the same in both. Shadows are on to begin with, so arrows and shapes are saved with the soft shadow you saw in the editor; turn it off with the new button.
  • Loupe remembers the font, weight and shadow you picked for the next screenshot.
