Loupe 1.0.11

  • Draw over blurs, pixelations and redactions. With a drawing tool, starting a new arrow or shape inside a blurred, pixelated or redacted area now draws it, instead of dragging that area away. Grab a region by its edge to move it, or use the Select tool to move it from anywhere inside.
  • Switching tools starts fresh. Picking a drawing tool deselects the shape you just drew, so your next drag draws the new shape instead of resizing the last one.
